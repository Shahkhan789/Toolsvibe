Tips for Better Results

  • Use clear, high-contrast images with readable text
  • Position text horizontally for best recognition accuracy
  • Ensure good lighting and minimal shadows in your images
  • Crop the image to focus on the text area you want to extract
  • For handwritten text, use clear printing if possible
  • Higher resolution images generally produce better results
